Scan & go

Point, scan, done.

Snap a photo of the prescription and ZenBell fills in the medicine, dose, and how often it's given. All read on-device — nothing is uploaded anywhere to make this work. And since text recognition isn't perfect, every field stays editable before anything is saved or a single reminder is scheduled.

Dose tracking

A checklist, not a guess.

Every medicine gets a day strip and a row of today's doses — given, missed, or still coming. Mark a dose given in one tap, straight from the reminder notification or the home screen widget, no app-opening required.

Fever & symptoms

Not just a number — the pattern.

Log a temperature and ZenBell tells you plainly whether it's normal, mild, or high. A full history grouped by day means you can actually see whether things are getting better or worse, not just stare at one isolated reading.

Six themes. Light and dark, each.

Pick a palette that fits your kid, or leave it on System and let it follow your phone.

The part I actually care about

No account. No cloud. No ads.

There's no backend for ZenBell to talk to, even if it wanted to. A sick kid's medical info is about as sensitive as data gets, and it stays exactly where you typed it: on your phone.

  • Everything stored locally — Room on Android, SwiftData on iOS
  • No account or sign-in, ever
  • No analytics SDKs, no ad trackers
  • Prescription scanning runs on-device, no cloud AI call
  • One opt-in exception: pasting a tricky prescription into an AI app of your choice — off by default, your call every time
